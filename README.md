An absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents: Nap-as-a-Service —
an AI that takes your 3pm meeting, naps through it, and wakes up with
notes so good your boss promotes you.

## The entry

**Nap-as-a-Service: an AI that takes your 3pm meeting, naps through it, and wakes up with notes so good your boss promotes you.**

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). No network, no randomness, no
arguments: the entry is fixed, so every run produces the same bytes.
